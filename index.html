Why Kress Leads

Technology Built Different

RTKn navigation isn't just a feature — it's an infrastructure advantage that makes every other robotic mower look like last decade's product.

01

RTKn Satellite Navigation

Real-Time Kinematic navigation using a professional network of local antennas delivers centimeter-level GPS accuracy. No base station to install on your roof. No buried boundary wires. Just precision, every pass.

02

Obstacle Avoidance (OAS)

The patented Obstacle Avoidance System detects hazards — toys, pets, people — without physical contact. On the KR800 Voyager: cameras, LiDAR, and ultrasonic sensors create 360° environmental awareness at all times.

03

ZeroTrim Edge Cutting

Cuts precisely to the perimeter, eliminating follow-up string trimming along most edges. Less labor, cleaner results, happier clients — and less noise from supplemental equipment.

04

8-Minute CyberSystem Battery

The fastest battery recharge in professional outdoor power equipment. The CyberPack goes 0→100% in 8 minutes via CyberTank. A crew can swap batteries on-site faster than it takes to fill a gas tank.

05

TeamWork Multi-Mower Coordination

Deploy multiple Kress mowers on a single large property and they coordinate autonomously — no overlapping coverage, no manual zoning. Scalable from residential estates to commercial campuses.

06

Fleet & Remote Management

The Kress Commercial platform provides real-time visibility across your entire mower fleet — machine status, activity tracking, diagnostics, and alerts — from any device, anywhere. Manage dozens of properties from your phone.
